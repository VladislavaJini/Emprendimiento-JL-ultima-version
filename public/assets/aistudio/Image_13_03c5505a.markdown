## Brand & Style

This design system translates the raw, physical precision of custom carpentry into a digital experience with a warm Scandinavian atelier aesthetic. It serves custom fabricators, master carpenters, architectural woodworkers, and discerning clients who value material honesty, joinery precision, and timeless craftsmanship.

The visual language balances organic warmth with architectural rigor:
- **Atmospheric Warmth:** Soft dark tones and natural charcoal paper replace stark clinical whites, creating an immersive, focused workshop atmosphere.
- **Architectural Clarity:** Geometric typographic discipline creates sharp contrast against soft, pill-shaped interactions and rounded material cards.
- **Tactile Honesty:** Surfaces evoke fine timber, honed stone, and cast terracotta through subtle tonal stacking and diffuse ambient shadows rather than synthetic gradients or noisy skeumorphic textures.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the workshop: terracotta clay markings, charred oak, kiln-dried birch, and natural tracing linen, optimized for a rich dark mode environment.

- **Primary (`#E87A50`):** Terracotta. Used for focal callouts, active state indicators, critical workshop milestones, and key action anchors.
- **Secondary (`#1A1A1A`):** Deep Charred Black. Provides high-contrast anchor typography, crisp framing elements, and grounded call-to-actions.
- **Tertiary (`#717171`):** Muted Slate / Steel. Serves as secondary containment surfaces, button bases, input troughs, and dividers.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#A1A1A1`):** Industrial Neutral Grey. Forms foundational structural elements and balanced mid-tones, mitigating eye fatigue.
- **Pure Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** Reserved for elevated tactile cards, floating sheets, and focus components to create natural luminosity against the dark canvas.

## Typography

Typography pairs the structural, geometric presence of **Epilogue** for architectural headings with the clean, neutral legibility of **DM Sans** for utilitarian data, dimensioning, specifications, and long-form narrative.

- **Headlines:** Set in Epilogue with snug letter-spacing to echo structural joinery and poster typography.
- **Body & Labels:** Set in DM Sans with generous optical line heights for field use on handheld devices and tablet workshop docks.
- **Numbers & Metrics:** Woodworking requires rapid metric comprehension; all key dimensions, lumber grades, and tolerances utilize tabular numerals within DM Sans for strict vertical alignment.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is governed by an 8-point base grid system, delivering rhythmic predictability across viewports:

- **Mobile (under 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `margin: 1.25rem` and `gutter: 1rem`. Bottom navigation bars and floating dock containers anchor touch targets within thumb reach.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout accommodating dual split-panes (e.g., cut lists alongside project blueprints).
- **Desktop (above 1024px):** 12-column fixed-max layout capped at 1280px with `margin-desktop: 3rem` to maintain comfortable eye-tracking across multi-metric atelier dashboards.
- **Component Breathing Room:** High component-level padding (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) inside cards preserves a calm, uncluttered workshop environment.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through soft, warm tonal stacking layered with dark ambient shadows, avoiding sterile grey drops.

- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Ground plane set in deep dark canvas tones.
- **Level 1 (Recessed Well):** Inset controls, search inputs, and inactive meters sit flush or slightly pressed into the background using `#717171` without borders.
- **Level 2 (Cards & Panels):** Raised surfaces cast an ambient shadow tinted with warm umber: `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.36), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.2)`.
- **Level 3 (Floating Docks & Sheets):** Interactive navigation docks and overlay modal cards feature increased elevation: `box-shadow: 0 16px 36px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.3)`.

## Shapes

The shape system blends structured planar cards with soft, touch-friendly rounded accents reflecting hand-sanded timber edges with a subtle, modern radius.

- **Base Radius (`rounded`, 0.25rem / 4px):** Standard containers, small thumbnail previews, inputs, and badges.
- **Large Radius (`rounded-lg`, 0.5rem / 8px):** Primary content cards, material panels, and bottom sheets.
- **Extra Large Radius (`rounded-xl`, 0.75rem / 12px):** Modal sheets, hero containers, and elevated showcase cards.
- **Pill Radius (`rounded-full`, 9999px):** Applied to primary action buttons, filter tags, chip indicators, and tactile navigation pods to communicate instantaneous pressability.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Full pill (`rounded-full`), deep charred black background (`#1A1A1A`) with pure white text, or terracotta (`#E87A50`) for key actions. Generous horizontal padding (`space-xl`), height 48px.
- **Secondary:** Muted slate fill (`#717171`), text `#1A1A1A`, full pill shape. No border.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Transparent base, `#1A1A1A` text with subtle hover tinting in `rgba(232, 122, 80, 0.08)`.

### Cards & Panels
- **Material Card:** Surface `#1a1c1c`, `rounded-xl`, subtle terracotta ambient shadow. Padded with `space-lg`.
- **Inset Stat Card:** Muted slate background `#717171`, `rounded-lg`, internal spacing `space-md`, devoid of outer shadows to convey an integrated workbench meter.

### Chips & Filters
- **Category Chips:** Inactive chips feature a slate background (`#717171`) with `#1A1A1A` text and optional timber/material iconography.
- **Active State:** Terracotta background (`#E87A50`) with white text and smooth spring transitions. Full pill shape.

### Input Fields & Search
- **Search Bar:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), surface background with soft ambient shadow or `#717171` flush surface. Prefix search icon in `#1A1A1A`, placeholder text in muted umber charcoal.
- **Form Inputs:** Muted slate fill (`#717171`), `rounded-lg` (8px), no border until focused. Focus ring: 2px solid `#E87A50` with 0px offset.

### Selection Controls
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 20px controls with 4px border radius for checkboxes and circular for radios. Inactive: `#717171` with a 1.5px soft charcoal edge. Checked: solid `#E87A50` with pure white check/bullet.

### Progress & Measurement Indicators
- **Radial & Arc Gauges:** Segmented block meters inspired by woodworking gauge dials. Active increments transition smoothly from soft peach into solid `#E87A50`, while empty steps remain muted slate `#717171`.
